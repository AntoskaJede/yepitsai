# YepIts.ai — Product Hunt Launch

## Tagline
Turn any YouTube video into a 2-minute read

## Description

YepIts.ai is an AI-powered tool that instantly summarizes any YouTube video. Paste a link, get a concise summary with key takeaways and clickable timestamps.

**Perfect for:**
- Students summarizing long lectures
- Podcast fans who are short on time
- Researchers scanning conference talks
- Newsletter writers pulling quotes from videos
- Anyone who values their time

**How it works:**
1. Paste a YouTube URL
2. AI reads the transcript and writes a 2-3 paragraph summary
3. Get 3-5 key takeaways and timestamps for the most important moments
4. Copy the whole thing with one click

**Free tier:** 3 summaries per day, videos up to 10 minutes
**Pro tier ($7/mo):** Unlimited summaries, any video length, export, no branding

No download. No browser extension. No credit card to start. Just paste a link.
