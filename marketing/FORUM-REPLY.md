**Reply:**

Hey, I've been down this same rabbit road. Tried Eightify (paywall after 3 summaries), NoteGPT (10/mo limit), and the manual transcript + ChatGPT route (works but tedious).

Recently started using YepIts.ai (https://yepits.ai) — paste the URL, get a summary with key takeaways and timestamps. Uses Claude under the hood so it handles jargon better than the cheaper tools. Free tier is 3/day, Pro is $7/mo which is cheaper than most options listed here.

What I like: it actually gives you clickable timestamps so you can jump to specific points, and the summaries don't cut off on longer videos. The "hallucinated structure" problem someone mentioned above is real with GPT-based tools — Claude seems better at sticking to what was actually said.

No Chrome extension privacy concerns either since it's a web app, not a browser extension that tracks your browsing.

Might be worth a shot if you're hitting paywalls on the other tools.
