Last updated: June 18, 2026

How YepIts.ai Works

YepIts.ai generates AI summaries of publicly available YouTube video transcripts. We do not host, download, reproduce, or distribute video content. We only process publicly accessible transcript text to create original summary content.

Copyright Concerns

If you believe that a summary generated by YepIts.ai infringes your copyright, please contact us. We respect intellectual property rights and will promptly address legitimate concerns.

Filing a DMCA Notice

To file a copyright complaint, send an email to pava@yepits.ai with the following information:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • The specific YouTube URL/summary in question
  • Proof that you own or are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright holder
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is unauthorized
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Our Response

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will remove the summarized content in question within 48 hours. We may also restrict summarization of specific videos or channels if requested by the copyright holder.

Counter-Notice

If you believe content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice with the same information above, plus consent to local court jurisdiction.

Repeat Infringers

We will terminate accounts of users who repeatedly use the Service to infringe copyright.
