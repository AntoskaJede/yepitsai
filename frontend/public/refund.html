Refund Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Pro Subscription Refunds

We want you to be satisfied with YepIts.ai Pro. If you're not happy, we offer refunds under the following conditions:

  • Within 7 days of payment: Full refund if you've used fewer than 10 summaries in the current billing period.
  • After 7 days: No refund for the current billing period, but you can cancel to prevent future charges.
  • Cancellation: Cancel anytime from your account or by emailing us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep Pro access until then.

How to Request a Refund

Email pava@yepits.ai with:

  • Your account email
  • The date of the charge
  • "Refund request" in the subject line

Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to your original payment method.

Free Tier

The free tier costs nothing, so no refunds apply. You can stop using it at any time.
