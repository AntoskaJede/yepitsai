Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how YepIts.ai uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit.

Cookies We Use

Essential cookies (required):

  • Authentication token: Keeps you logged in to your account. Without this cookie, you would need to log in on every page.

That's it. We use one essential cookie.

Cookies We Do NOT Use

  • ❌ No advertising cookies (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • ❌ No analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • ❌ No third-party tracking cookies
  • ❌ No social media cookies

Managing Cookies

Since we only use an essential authentication cookie, there is nothing to opt out of. If you clear your cookies, you will simply be logged out and need to sign in again.

Changes

If we add cookies in the future, we will update this policy and notify you.
