Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how YepIts.ai uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit.

Cookies We Use

Essential cookies (required):

  • Authentication token: Keeps you logged in to your account. Without this, you would need to log in on every page.

First-party analytics cookies:

  • yi_vid: A random visitor ID so we can count how many people visit. Stored for one year. It contains nothing about you and is never shared.
  • yi_attr: If you arrived through a link with campaign tags (for example from Reddit or a newsletter), this remembers which channel brought you here. Stored for 30 days.

These two cookies are set and read only by yepits.ai. They exist so we can see whether anyone visits and which channels are worth our time. They do not follow you to other websites.

Cookies We Do NOT Use

  • ❌ No advertising cookies (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • ❌ No third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • ❌ No third-party tracking cookies
  • ❌ No social media cookies

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Clearing them logs you out and resets the visitor ID; the site keeps working either way.

Changes

If we add cookies in the future, we will update this policy and notify you.
